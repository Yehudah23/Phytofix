Phytofix Ltd.

Nature.

Impact.

Phytofix Ltd. is a Nigerian phytopharmaceutical and botanical innovation company founded to bridge the gap between scientific research and practical healthcare solutions through the development of research based botanical products.

The company focuses on the scientific discovery, formulation, development, and commercialization of evidence informed botanical products derived from medicinal plants and other natural resources.

✦

Our approach

Transforming promising plant based discoveries into standardized, safe, effective, and market ready wellness products.

The company combines phytochemistry, pharmaceutical biochemistry, pharmacology, and modern scientific approaches to transform promising plant based discoveries into standardized, safe, effective, and market ready wellness products.

Our vision

To become a leading African phytopharmaceutical company translating indigenous botanical knowledge and scientific research into globally relevant health and wellness solutions.

Our mission

To develop and commercialize scientifically researched, innovative, standardized, and quality driven botanical products that promote health, wellness, and improved quality of life.

✦

Core philosophy

Nature • Science • Impact

Driven by the philosophy “Nature. Science. Impact.”, Phytofix Ltd. seeks to harness Africa’s rich medicinal plant biodiversity while applying rigorous scientific principles to product development. The company’s goal is not simply to produce herbal products, but to translate research findings into innovative botanical solutions with measurable health and economic impact.

Building what comes next

A credible African enterprise with a global outlook.

Phytofix Ltd. is committed to building a credible African phytopharmaceutical enterprise with the capacity to serve the Nigerian, African, and global markets, while contributing to research commercialization, local pharmaceutical innovation, job creation, and the development of sustainable value chains for medicinal plants.
